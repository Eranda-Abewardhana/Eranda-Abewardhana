### 2. Explainable Deep Learning for Genomic Variant Prediction

**Deep Learning | Bioinformatics | PyTorch | Transformers | Captum**

A research project developed as my final-year thesis at the University of Moratuwa, focusing on predicting the pathogenic impact of genomic single-nucleotide variants (SNVs).

- Designed a mutation-aware dual-input deep learning architecture processing reference (REF) and alternative (ALT) genomic sequences.
- Combined Multi-Scale CNNs and Transformer Encoders to capture local sequence patterns and long-range dependencies.
- Developed a difference-learning module to identify mutation-specific information.
- Trained and evaluated the framework using approximately 714K ClinVar records mapped to GRCh38.
- Achieved a **0.9465 ROC-AUC** on the held-out test set.
- Applied Explainable AI techniques including Integrated Gradients, Guided AbsoluteGrad, and In-silico Mutagenesis using Captum.
